The story of Le & Associates (L&A)

Le & Associates (L&A) is one of Vietnam's leading companies in HR Outsourcing, HRIS, and human capital management consulting, with more than 25 years of operation and a team of over 200 people. Over two and a half decades of working with hundreds of local and international businesses, L&A has built an invaluable asset: a practical knowledge base in human resource management, refined by its C-level team through projects, clients, and complex business challenges. But that large body of know-how also created a new challenge.

The challenge: 25 years of expertise, but not yet digitalized properly

As the company continued to scale its workforce outsourcing operations, onboarding new employees became increasingly overloaded. Ms. Le, CEO of L&A, recognized a clear paradox: "L&A owns 25 years of valuable experience, but much of that knowledge still sits inside the minds of managers. It has not yet been systemized into a structured training program."

The traditional offline training model, repeated year after year, could only reach a small group of learners who attended in person. It was becoming a recurring cost that did not accumulate into a long-term organizational asset. With a busy C-level team, continuously finding time to repeat training sessions for every new employee cohort was no longer sustainable.

L&A's 25 years of expertise faced the risk of being lost whenever experienced employees left, while the company still lacked a system to store, standardize, and reuse lessons for future learner groups.

The solution: From "retelling" knowledge to "leaving" knowledge behind with Nesso

Nesso worked with L&A not only as a software provider, but as a co-creation partner. The team directly interviewed L&A's C-level leaders to extract their practical knowledge, then combined that insight with the company's internal documents to transform 15 core lessons from oral knowledge-sharing into standardized LMS courses.

The entire process, from storyboard development and AI-powered content production to SCORM packaging and learning interface customization, was delivered end to end by Nesso. This allowed L&A to digitalize training without having to build all production capability from scratch.

The biggest difference was not the technology itself, but Nesso's approach. Technology is only the tool; Customer Success is what determines whether an LMS can truly become active inside an organization. From the first consulting session, Nesso focused on solving Ms. Le's real concern: how to extract 25 years of expertise quickly and accurately without disrupting the daily work of the leadership team.

The result: From a repeated cost to an asset that grows over time

By moving 15 core lessons onto the LMS, L&A's initial investment was equivalent to less than 14 months of training costs under the old offline model. In other words, the company could recover its investment in just over one year. From that point onward, the entire learning content library belongs to L&A permanently. It can be reused for every new employee cohort, every year, with unlimited learning sessions and without additional trainer or classroom organization costs.

Because the learning content can be reused without limit, the return on investment grows over time. After five years of operation, this one-time investment helps L&A reduce total training costs by approximately 70% compared with maintaining the offline model.

More than 200 L&A employees can now access the full set of 15 courses at any time. At the same time, the leadership team can track completion rates and engagement levels through a management dashboard, something 25 years of oral training had never been able to provide.

"Digital training transformation is not about throwing away 25 years of experience and starting again from software. It is about making sure that knowledge no longer depends on the memory of a few people, but becomes a shared asset the entire organization can learn from at any time."

That is the insight Nesso and L&A reached together, and it is now being gradually turned into reality through L&A's digital training transformation journey.
